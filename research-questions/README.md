# Research Questions

This directory documents the research questions that emerge from the
literature review and are investigated through empirical experimentation.

The questions will evolve as the research progresses.

## Current Research Direction

AI-driven decision-making in complex and uncertain environments.

## Core Research Themes

- Decision intelligence
- Reinforcement learning
- Multi-agent systems
- Uncertainty-aware AI
- Robust decision-making
- Human-AI decision-making

## Research Process

Literature
→ Research Gap
→ Research Question
→ Hypothesis
→ Methodology
→ Experiment
→ Results
→ Research Contribution
