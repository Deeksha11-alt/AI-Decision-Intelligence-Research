# RQ01 — AI Decision-Making Under Uncertainty

## Working Research Question

How can AI systems make robust decisions when the underlying state
of an environment is uncertain or only partially observable?

## Motivation

Real-world decision environments rarely provide complete,
perfectly reliable information.

AI systems may need to make decisions using incomplete,
noisy, delayed, or ambiguous information.

This creates a challenge for decision-making systems that must
balance prediction, uncertainty, and action.

## Research Areas

- Reinforcement learning
- Multi-agent systems
- Uncertainty estimation
- Robust decision-making
- Decision intelligence

## Potential Application Context

Financial and payment systems provide an initial environment for
studying decision-making under uncertainty.

## Initial Hypothesis

Explicitly representing uncertainty may improve the robustness
of AI decision-making compared with approaches that assume
complete or accurately observed environmental states.

## Questions to Investigate

1. How should uncertainty be represented in an AI decision system?

2. How does uncertainty affect decision quality?

3. How does uncertainty affect coordination between multiple agents?

4. Can disagreement between agents provide a useful signal of uncertainty?

5. How robust are different decision-making approaches under
   distribution shift?

## Potential Experimental Direction

Compare decision-making performance under:

1. Complete information
2. Partial information
3. Noisy observations
4. Distribution shift

Evaluate:

- Decision quality
- Robustness
- Reward/performance
- Error rate
- Agent coordination

## Current Status

Working research question.

The question will be refined through literature review and
experimental investigation.
